1. Introduction & Overview

Ruya Technologies built Ruya POS as an offline-first point of sale and inventory management app for independent retailers, wholesale distributors, and shopkeepers. We respect your privacy and believe your sales numbers and customer records belong strictly to you.

2. Core Architecture: Offline-First Principle

Ruya POS is engineered with an offline-first local database architecture:

  • Local Storage: All business records (products, customer ledgers, supplier bills, transactions, invoices, expenses) are stored securely on your physical device in SQLite.
  • Full Offline Usability: You can operate Ruya POS indefinitely without an internet connection or cloud account.
  • Total Ownership: You retain complete control over your store's financial records.

3. Information We Collect

A. Business Operational Records (Stored Locally on Device)

Inventory counts, product pricing, customer balances, supplier ledgers, sales orders, and receipt configurations are stored locally on your device.

B. Account & Cloud Synchronization Data (Optional)

If you activate Cloud Sync, your account email and database records are synchronized to secure PostgreSQL cloud servers (Supabase) protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

C. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Subscriptions and license purchases are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing. We never collect, receive, or store credit card, debit card, or banking details.

D. Device Permissions

  • Camera: Used exclusively for real-time barcode scanning. No photos or video feeds are captured or stored.
  • Bluetooth / Local Network: Used to connect to ESC/POS thermal receipt printers.
  • Biometrics: Used to unlock your cashier shift via device hardware enclave. Biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Notifications: Used for low-stock alerts and daily register closing reminders.

5. Data Security & Storage Safeguards

We implement multi-layered security safeguards, including encrypted AES-256 local database backups, PostgreSQL Row Level Security, salt-hashed PIN access controls, and automatic inactivity lockouts.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

You may wipe local data at any time via Settings -> Advanced -> Reset / Clear Database.
